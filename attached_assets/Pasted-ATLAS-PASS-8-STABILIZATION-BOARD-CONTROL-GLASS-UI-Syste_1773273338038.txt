====================================================
1. CRITICAL BUG — GOOGLE NEWS ENTITY DETECTION
====================================================

Problem:
When clicking ANALYZE on documents, entity extraction often produces a single entity:

"Google News"

This is incorrect and usually caused by:
• parsing the Google News RSS wrapper instead of the article
• extracting metadata instead of article body
• failing to follow the redirect to the real source
• incomplete HTML extraction

Fix requirements:

1. Detect Google News wrapper URLs.
2. Resolve the original article URL when possible.
3. Extract the real article body.

If the body cannot be retrieved:
- mark extraction as PARTIAL
- do NOT generate entities from metadata blocks.

Entity extraction should only run on real article text.
