T002 — ENTITY INTELLIGENCE PURGE
Fix the brain.
Any entity that is clearly not a real investigative actor/institution/program/location tied to the case must be blocked or heavily penalized.
Specifically harden against:
- celebrity/news side-story entities
- generic nouns and slogans
- tourism, entertainment, sports, media filler
- irrelevant geographic names
- government acronyms that appear in unrelated side stories
- nonprofit/program names unrelated to the anchor target
- “good news”, “analysis”, “overview”, “homepage”, “community room news”, etc.

Implement:
- stronger anchor proximity scoring
- stronger case-domain scoring
- source-title vs body weighting
- off-topic side-story penalty
- “cross-document aligned relevance” requirement before promotion
- entity blacklist/skip expansion
- institution sanity check: if org/entity has no semantic relation to target, downgrade or reject
- person/org/location/program type correction before scoring
- require at least one of:
  a) appears in multiple aligned docs
  b) appears near target anchor + funding/investigative language
  c) appears in title/lead of a CORE aligned doc
